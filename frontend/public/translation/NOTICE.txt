Simplified Chinese to English model, Mozilla Translations release 2.1, MPL 2.0.
Source: https://github.com/mozilla/translations
Model distribution: https://firefox.settings.services.mozilla.com/v1/buckets/main/collections/translations-models/records?fromLang=zh-Hans&toLang=en
Model, vocabulary and lexical shortlist checksums are in registry.json.
The model is split into three consecutive parts for transport. Concatenating the parts restores the original.
