# 00 · Product brief — StayCraft concept-demo promo (EN, 60s)

> Lead (2026-11-13). Scope: one 60-second English product film built on the new
> Gemini concept demo. Old films stay untouched; this is a new project folder.

## Product
- **UI baseline**: frontend/public/concept/index.html + demo.js (final Gemini source;
  Noto Sans SC + Outfit; violet #6554c7; radius 18; flat light cards).
- **What it is**: an AI decision workbench for independent stays — revenue data,
  AI diagnosis, market quotes and promotion copy in one workspace.
- **Demo nature**: static concept demo, fictional sample data, no API/auth; visits at
  https://staycraft.online/try.

## Audience / job
- Independent homestay / small-stay operators. The film sells one promise:
  **evidence-backed decisions, not a dashboard tour.**
- The film must make three functions legible in one pass: **AI diagnosis (Decision
  Workbench)**, **Business Data & Bookings**, **Nearby Market Research**.

## Frozen facts (from the EN demo, 2026-11-10 → 11-16)
- Completed room revenue **¥18,450** (+14.2%); sold room nights **42**;
  achieved room rate **¥439.29**; future booked value **¥12,800** (24/35 nights).
- Occupancy **68.6%** (24/35, 11 open); 4 room-nights from the 80% target;
  peer average 62%.
- Core judgment: Saturday **sold out**, Sunday **1 room left** → test a **¥40**
  Sunday rate increase; nearby Saturday median **¥540** (+25.5% vs midweek).
- Daily figures: 11/14 Sat **100%** (5 booked / 0 open / ¥2,950), 11/15 Sun **80%**
  (4 booked / 1 open / ¥2,480); weekday nights 15, weekend nights 9.
- Market: sample median **¥540/night**; middle 50% band **¥488–¥594**; listed rates
  **¥420–¥580**; 4 stays / 4 valid quotes; advice "keep Saturday firm, test
  ¥520–¥540 on the remaining Sunday room".
- Caveat: concept demo, fictional data; no real OTA/PMS/payment integration.

## Deliverable
- 60.0s, 1920×1080, 30fps, 1800 frames, H.264 + AAC.
- English only; **no VO, no burned subtitles** (user adds them later with GPT).
- BGM + SFX included; a **no-BGM variant** (SFX only) is delivered for VO mixing.
- QR end card → https://staycraft.online (English cut only).
- Old Chinese/English films remain unchanged.

## Constraints
- Never modify frontend sources or the old video projects.
- No Chinese readable in the picture (demo EN mode has a few untranslated labels;
  these are localized at capture time only, data untouched — documented in
  capture/localization notes).
- Numbers must match the captured demo exactly; no invented features.
- Creative team: creative-director (direction + storyboard), copywriter (screen
  copy), motion-a / motion-b / motion-c (shots), Lead (capture, integration,
  render, QA).
