# 课程背景

## 项目要求

This module is about digital entrepreneurship: taking an idea from concept to a working digital product \(typically an app\) using modern tools like no\-code platforms and AI agents, and validating it with real users\. Projects must address a real problem and demonstrate technical depth, not just static pages or simple features\.

A project must result in： Working digital product \(app/dashboard/web tool\) with user\-facing interface, user input, at least five screens, user authentication, three core features, support for 10\+ real users

It should let you demonstrate：

- Develop a comprehensive understanding of cutting\-edge technologies pivotal in digital startup ecosystems, discerning their application and significance within entrepreneurial ventures\. — The project must involve researching and applying cutting\-edge technologies \(e\.g\., AI, AI agents, no\-code/low\-code\) and articulating their significance in the startup context, as evidenced in reports and checkpoints\.

- Identify and analyze technological integration within startups, employing diverse methodologies and tools that optimize technological resources\. — The project must demonstrate selection and integration of appropriate tech stack and tools, with rationale and analysis of how they optimize resources, as seen in tech stack decisions and reports\.

- Cultivate emotional intelligence and resilience, fostering the ability to navigate the emotional highs and lows inherent in digital entrepreneurship\. — The project must involve navigating challenges, setbacks, and teamwork dynamics, with evidence of resilience and emotional intelligence in weekly logs, reflections, and mentor interactions\.

- Conceptualize and implement innovative solutions by integrating technologies including AI agents and intelligent automation\. — The project must include conceptualizing and building a solution that integrates AI agents or intelligent automation as core features, as required in the building phase\.

- Attain proficiency in interactive simulations and digital tools, applying technological insights to entrepreneurial challenges through real\-world practice\. — The project must involve hands\-on use of digital tools \(e\.g\., prototyping, no\-code platforms\) and applying them to real\-world problems, demonstrated through the working prototype and user testing\.

## 我们选择的课题（从课程给出的现有里项目中选择）

以下是项目说明原文：

> **Reimagining the Homestay Industry: Seasonality, AI Agents, and Intelligent Decision Support**
> 
> PartnerTengchong Xinling Fuxi Tower Hotel Management腾冲市心灵复苏福熙楼酒店有限公司
> 
> Problem / challenge description
> 
> Tengchong Xinling Fuxi Tower Hotel Management Co\., Ltd\. \(hereinafter referred to as "the Company"\) was established on May 10, 2018, with its registered office in Heshun Town, Tengchong City, Yunnan Province, China, and a registered capital of RMB 500,000\. The Company is a professional service enterprise specializing in homestay \(minshuku\) operation and management\. Currently, it manages and operates over 30 homestay properties with nearly 1,000 guest rooms across regions including Yunnan, Tibet, Xinjiang, Sichuan\-Chongqing, Wuxi, Hangzhou, Fuzhou, and Tangshan\. The Company adheres to a brand positioning of professionalism, standardization, and refinement, dedicated to providing one\-stop operation and management solutions for homestay investors and operators to enhance industry quality\. Its core business comprises three main segments: First, OTA \(Online Travel Agency\) operation services, covering listing management, order processing, pricing strategy optimization, promotional campaigns, and review management to boost exposure and conversion rates\. Second, full\-cycle management and trusteeship services, spanning project positioning, brand planning, operational system setup, service standard formulation, and team building\. Third, professional training programs for homestay practitioners, including service etiquette, housekeeping management, online marketing skills, customer communication, and emergency response, aiming to cultivate qualified talent for the industry\.
> 
> Where could this go?
> 
> These are not recommendations and none is better than another — they're here to show how differently this brief could be answered\. Your team's own direction should come from your research, not this list\.
> 
> 1\. Predictive analytics platform
> 
> A data\-driven tool that ingests historical booking, pricing, and local event data to forecast demand patterns and suggest dynamic pricing or staffing adjustments for homestay operators, helping them navigate weak peak seasons\.
> 
> 2\. AI\-agent orchestration system
> 
> A suite of AI agents that automate routine operations such as guest inquiries, review responses, and dynamic listing updates, potentially integrated with OTA platforms to enhance efficiency and enable 'super individual' management\.
> 
> 3\. Knowledge graph and research agent
> 
> An industry\-specific research agent that constructs a knowledge graph of homestay regulations, market trends, and best practices, and provides decision support through automated data collection and analysis, bridging information gaps for fragmented operators\.
> 
> 4\. Collaborative community platform
> 
> A platform that connects homestay owners, managers, and service providers to share resources, benchmark performance, and coordinate on local marketing or purchasing, fostering resilience through collective action against seasonality\.
> 
> 5\. Training and simulation environment
> 
> An interactive simulation tool that trains homestay practitioners in cycle literacy and anti\-cyclical strategies, using realistic scenarios and AI\-driven feedback to build skills for managing demand fluctuations\.
> 
> **WHAT ANY SOLUTION MUST SATISFY**
> 
> **Address at least one of the three research topics: weak peak season analysis, AI agent applications, or industry\-specific research agent construction\.**
> 
> **Integrate AI agents or intelligent automation in the proposed solution, as per course learning outcomes\.**
> 
> **Demonstrate understanding of digital startup ecosystems and technological integration within entrepreneurial ventures\.**
> 
> **Be feasible to prototype and present at a demo day within the course timeline\.**
> 
> **Consider the fragmented nature of the homestay industry and the need for actionable intelligence from abundant information\.**
> 
> **Align with the company's brand positioning of professionalism, standardization, and refinement\.**
> 
> **Incorporate emotional intelligence and resilience considerations, especially in user experience or change management aspects\.**
> 
> FIND THESE OUT BEFORE YOU CHOOSE
> 
> 1\. What specific data sources are available for homestay operations, and how can they be accessed ethically and legally?
> 
> 2\. How do homestay operators currently make decisions during weak peak seasons, and what information gaps do they perceive?
> 
> 3\. What are the key pain points in OTA operations that AI agents could address without alienating channel partners?
> 
> 4\. How can the solution ensure adoption by small\-scale operators with limited technical literacy?
> 
> 5\. What ethical and governance frameworks should guide the deployment of AI agents in homestay operations, particularly regarding guest data and workforce impact?
> 
> Expected Outcomes \& Deliverables:
> 
> This project comprises three interconnected research topics\. The first examines "Weak Peak Season" as a structural industry inflection point, analyzing macro\-level supply\-demand reversals, micro\-level anti\-cyclical strategies for individual properties, and cross\-disciplinary frameworks including dual\-factor prediction models and cycle literacy among practitioners\. The second explores AI agent \("Lobster"\) applications in homestay operations, covering operational efficiency gains through "super individual" models, ecosystem impacts on booking channels and competitive dynamics, workforce transformation, and ethical governance\. The third focuses on constructing an industry\-specific research agent with a three\-layer architecture—knowledge graph construction, automated multi\-source data collection and analysis, and empirical validation of decision\-support effectiveness—aiming to bridge the gap between information abundance and actionable intelligence in the fragmented homestay sector\.
